Speaking Clubs

Who Leads Our Speaking Clubs?

Our speaking clubs are led by experienced professionals and native speakers focused on real communication, confidence and practical English for modern life.

Cory

Cory is a native English speaker from Texas with a warm Southern accent. He believes students should start speaking immediately, even with mistakes, because confidence and fluency grow naturally through real conversation.

Inspired by the wisdom of his grandmother, Cory creates a supportive and motivating atmosphere in every speaking club.

Marta

Marta is an experienced HR professional who worked at Google in Dublin in a senior position and lived in Ireland for over 10 years.

She specializes in business English, interview preparation, and professional communication, and also hosts live meetings in Warsaw.

Learning System

Our Learning Methodology

We combine a structured academic approach with modern learning tools, interactive exercises, and real communication practice. Students are carefully placed into groups based on their goals and learning pace.

From the very first lesson, students start speaking and practicing useful language structures. All students also receive free access to speaking clubs with native speakers.
